## P00-04 · HOW TO PLAY
TEMPLATE: info
TEXT:
- How to play this guide
- 1. Pick a Recipe from the Menu. Recipes build on each other, so 01 → 05 is the best route.
- 2. Every Step follows the same cook-along: Mama's Problem → Predict → Try it → Look → Why it works → Use it → Taste Test.
- 3. Mini-games have six parts: 🎯 Goal · 🧺 Prep · 🔪 Task · 👀 Observe · 💭 Reflect · ⭐ Mama's Feedback.
- 4. Stamp your Score Card: Gold = right first time · Silver = right after a hint · Bronze = read the answer and redo it.
- 5. Finish a Recipe → unlock its page in the Recipe Book!
- Kitchen tools you need: pen, paper, a calculator, a phone or laptop with internet for the 🌐 Kitchen Lab activities (offline back-ups are always included).
IMG: Mama pointing / instruction pose; medal icons

## P00-05 · LEARNING GOALS & PREREQUISITES
TEMPLATE: info
TEXT:
- What you will be able to cook by the end
- 1. Name CPU, GPU, TPU/NPU and memory, and explain what each does for AI. (Recipe 01)
- 2. Explain how networks move AI data, and choose cloud vs edge using latency and bandwidth. (Recipe 02)
- 3. Describe how an agent learns from rewards, and explain exploration vs exploitation. (Recipe 03)
- 4. Compare cameras, LiDAR and ultrasonic sensors, and explain sensor fusion. (Recipe 04)
- 5. Explain how hardware, networks and software are combined and optimised at scale. (Recipe 05)
- Before you start (prerequisites): basic arithmetic; what a variable and a loop are; a rough idea of what a CPU and the internet are. No electronics or advanced maths needed.
- Who it is for: 1st/2nd-year CS & IT undergraduates. Time: about 1.5–2 hours per Recipe.
