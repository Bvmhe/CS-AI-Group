🥢 Kitchen Analogy
Rule Chef = a cook who always turns right at every corner. Map Chef = a cook with the floor plan. Learner Pup = a puppy rewarded with treats until it knows the way.
⚠️ Chef's Warning
None of the three “understands” the kitchen. Rule Chef applies a hand-written rule; Map Chef runs a search over a map it was given. Only the Learner Pup changes its behaviour from experience, by updating numbers in a table after every step (Recipe 03, Section 3.3).
🔬 The update the pup uses
Q(s,a) ← Q(s,a) + α · [ r + γ · maxₐ′ Q(s′,a′) − Q(s,a) ]
s = square (plus “cookie eaten?” when that option is on), a = move, r = reward, s′ = the square it lands on. Each practice run lasts at most 150 moves.
