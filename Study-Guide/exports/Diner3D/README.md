## How it works
- **The map is a 3D diner.** Each chapter is a room: Main Kitchen (Ch 1), Front Counter (Ch 2), BBQ Garden (Ch 3), Sushi Bar (Ch 4), Rooftop Banquet (Ch 5) and Centre Stage (Final Exam). Each lesson is a spot in its room.
- **Quick checks are a 2D serving game.** A customer orders a question. You do a quick cooking move (chop, stir, flip or pour), then serve the plate with the right answer.
- **Rush Hour** replaces the boss fights: 10 orders with patience bars and a trouble customer at the end.
- **Final Exam:** the Professor is the VIP food critic. He orders three courses and writes your review in *The Daily Wok*.
- **Regulars with perks:** Big Eater (double tips), Blocky Guy (easy true/false), Night Owl (night only, freezes patience), Hype Guy (moves the whole queue's mood) and Mystery Pork (rare, ×5 golden tip).

The game is inspired by Cooking Mama but uses only original art. The customer photos were supplied by the team, the cut-outs were made with OpenCV, and no AI image tools were used.
