# RESUBMISSION REPORT

## Revision Overview

We are resubmitting our module to address several weaknesses identified in our initial submission. In addition to the insufficient explanation of AI usage, we recognized that some sections lacked depth, certain concepts were not clearly connected, and the overall organization could be improved. These limitations affected the completeness, clarity, and effectiveness of our learning materials.

To address these issues, we conducted further research, refined our explanations, improved the content structure, and strengthened the connections between key concepts. We also incorporated AI into our evaluation process to identify missing information, assess content completeness, and obtain suggestions for improvement. Rather than relying entirely on AI, we critically reviewed its feedback and made our own decisions regarding the necessary revisions.

Through this resubmission, we aim to present a more comprehensive, well-organized, and informative module that better fulfills the project requirements and learning objectives while demonstrating a more meaningful and responsible use of AI.

This revision focuses on improving content depth, connections between topics, organization, research quality, and alignment with the learning objectives, rather than treating AI usage as the sole reason for resubmission. All reported changes should reflect the revisions actually completed by our group.

## Identifying Problems

### 1. Incomplete GitHub Repository

Our initial GitHub repository did not fully meet the project requirements, as several required files and sections were missing. This made the repository incomplete and potentially difficult to evaluate against the assignment criteria.

### 2. Disorganized File Structure

The files in our repository were not organized consistently. For example, image files were not stored in a dedicated folder, making the repository less structured and reducing its overall readability and maintainability.

### 3. Missing Content Review Section

Our initial submission did not include a dedicated section documenting the review and evaluation of the content. As a result, there was insufficient evidence of how we assessed the quality, completeness, and consistency of our module across multiple drafts.

---

## Solutions

### 1. Reviewing the Project Requirements and Previous Works

We revisited the project instructions on Lexue to ensure that we fully understood the required deliverables and evaluation criteria. We also reviewed previous years' projects to better understand how the requirements could be fulfilled and how our work could be improved. Based on this review, we identified missing components and determined which revisions were necessary to bring our submission into closer alignment with the project expectations.

### 2. Improving the GitHub Repository Organization

We reorganized our GitHub repository to make its structure clearer, more consistent, and easier to navigate. This included reviewing the existing files, identifying missing components, and arranging related materials into appropriate folders. For example, we planned a dedicated folder for image assets to separate them from the main content files. These improvements aim to make the repository easier to maintain, review, and use collaboratively.

### 3. Creating a Content Quality Review Document

We created a dedicated document to evaluate the quality of our content across multiple drafts. This document records the review process, identifies weaknesses in earlier versions, and explains the improvements made to address them. The evaluation focuses on factors such as accuracy, completeness, clarity, organization, and alignment with the learning objectives. We also used AI-generated feedback as a supplementary evaluation tool, critically assessing its suggestions before deciding which changes to implement. This process provides clearer evidence of our revisions and supports a more systematic approach to quality assurance.
