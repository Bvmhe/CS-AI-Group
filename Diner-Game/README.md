# 🍳 Fengwei · Diner World Tour (study game, preview)

A cooking-diner game for studying for Stage 2. Run Mama's diners around the world, serve customers before they lose patience, and earn big tips by answering questions from our Stage 2 Test Bank (60 questions across all five recipes of the Study Guide).

**To play:** download `HotWok_Diner_World_Tour_PREVIEW.html` and open it in any browser (phone sideways or computer). It's one file and works with no internet.

## What's inside

| Part | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| 🗺️ **5 diners** | Silicon Burger Cart (Recipe 01 · AI Hardware), Ping-Pong Noodle Bar (02 · Networks), Good Pup Café (03 · Behaviourist AI & RL), Sensor Sushi Train (04 · AI Sensors), Cloud Banquet Tower (05 · Integration & Optimisation). Each has 3 levels, and the questions come from that diner's recipe. |
| 🔥 **Real cooking** | Put food in the pans and watch it brown. Serve it when the ring turns green; leave it too long and it smokes, then burns. Pour drinks at the drink machine. Fast serves build combos. |
| 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Customers** | Regulars, kids and grandmas, plus 🎓 **The Professor (VIP)**, who pays triple and gives 💎 for a right answer, and 📹 **Big Bite Bao**, a live-streaming mukbang star who orders the whole menu. |
| 🧠 **Tip questions** | Happy customers ask True/False and multiple-choice questions. Every answer shows Mama's tip from the Test Bank. |
| 👹 **Mini bosses** | Each diner ends in a fighting-game showdown after closing time. Answer right to hit the boss; answer wrong and the boss hits Mama. Fast answers land a PERFECT critical strike. Graded S to D. |
| 🎓 **Final boss** | The Professor's Final Exam in 3 rounds: The Lecture (True/False), The Examination (multiple choice) and Final Grading (open questions with model answers, graded by yourself). Quick exam (14 Qs) or full exam (all 60). |
| 🛒 **Upgrades** | Turbo Wok, Warming Plates, Comfy Chairs, Golden Tip Jar, Extra Stove, Turbo Dispenser, Cute Décor, plus boss perks (Lucky Apron, Recipe Book, Kitchen Timer). |
| 🥚🍳🔥 **Difficulty** | Apprentice (beginner, no timers), Chef (normal) or Master Chef (expert, timed tip questions, coins ×1.5). |

Controls: tap or click. On a keyboard: **1 2** to cook, **3** to pour, **Q W E R** to serve from the pans, **A S** to serve drinks, and **1–4** to answer in boss fights. Progress saves in your browser.

Preview note: the map has a "Preview pass" box that unlocks every diner and boss so you can test them all.
